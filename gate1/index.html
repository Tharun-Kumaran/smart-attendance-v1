Why Replay Attacks Fail

WhatsApp / Codec Stripping: If someone records a voice memo or sends audio over WhatsApp, lossy compression completely cuts off everything above 16 kHz. Gate 1 detects absolute silence.

Physical Speaker Roll-Off: Phone speakers drop -30 dB at 18 kHz. They cannot reproduce enough ultrasound energy without creating severe sub-harmonic buzz.

Ephemeral Nonces: Tokens expire in seconds. Any pre-recorded sound is stamped with past time and rejected automatically.
